Our philosophy

Touch, done with intention.

Every session begins the same way: warm hands, warmed oil, and no clock in sight. We trained under Thai and Balinese masters who treat the body as a whole — not a list of symptoms — and we've kept that pace ever since opening our doors in Maitama.

Abuja moves quickly. Escape House doesn't. We keep our rooms few, our schedule unhurried, and our therapists trained for years, not weeks — so the hour you give us is returned in full.
